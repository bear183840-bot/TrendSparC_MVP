describe what a "future_business" (미래사업) question's report should
emphasize differently from other intents — e.g. which sections to
prioritize, what should be highlighted (forward-looking opportunities,
planned initiatives, market outlook), and any framing guidance for the
audience-adaptation step to use.

raw text as `ReportPlan.intent_emphasis`; nothing currently reads or acts
on it beyond that.
