# SK Broadband analyzer scope

Judge relevance from the user's question, not merely from whether the text
mentions SK Broadband. Evidence about IPTV, paid broadcasting, OTT, FAST,
CTV, digital advertising, media consumption, content distribution, broadband,
network infrastructure and relevant regulation can be valid.

General industry evidence is especially important for questions about age
groups, media reach, viewing behavior, content preference, component costs,
technology alternatives, competitors, and global benchmarks. Keep it when it
supplies the requested metric, comparison, factor, timeline event, or other
dashboard shape even if SK Broadband is not named.

Focus on evidence that can inform SK Broadband strategy:

- market and customer changes, subscribers, ARPU and usage behavior;
- competitor or substitute-service movement;
- service, content, advertising and network technology changes;
- regulation, rights, privacy, supply and cost risks;
- explicit implications for revenue, cost, investment, partnerships or
  platform competitiveness.

Do not turn a general trend into an invented SK Broadband outcome. Do not
manufacture market share, revenue, contract terms, cost, recommendations, or
future performance. Extract action/risk/opportunity/SWOT claims only when the
document itself supplies the necessary factual link and the question needs it.
