# SK hynix analyzer scope

Apply the shared evidence rules to SK hynix only. Focus on memory semiconductors
(HBM, DRAM, NAND), AI-memory demand, capacity and CAPEX, earnings, supply-chain
and customer developments, semiconductor competitors (including Samsung and
Micron), technology roadmaps, and applicable policy or export-control risks.

Do not broaden the analysis into foundry-process detail unless it materially
affects SK hynix's memory business. Keep HBM generations distinct and do not
state unconfirmed customer allocations, contract volumes, or prices as fact.
